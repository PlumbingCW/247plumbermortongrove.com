Using this website

This website gives general plumbing information and our contact details. It is not a substitute for a licensed plumber's inspection of your home. Articles describe common situations, and your home may differ. Do not use the website as a way to report an active emergency. Call (847) 281-8626.

Service requests

You request service by phone. A licensed plumber confirms the problem on site and gives you a clear price before work starts. You decide whether to go ahead. Same-day appointments are offered when available, and availability depends on call volume, weather and the work involved.

Permits and codes

Some plumbing work needs a permit from the village where the property sits. Permit requirements and fees are set by each municipality, and we may ask you to confirm details with the local building department. Work is performed to the plumbing code and local amendments that apply to your property.

Safety

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility from a safe place before you call us. If sewage or water is near electrical equipment, stay clear until it is safe.

Information on this site

We work to keep the information accurate, but it can change. Local figures such as utility reports, permit rules and village programs are published by the agencies that run them, and you should confirm current details with them.
