Tankless install and service

Tankless Water Heater Installation and Service in Morton Grove, IL

A tankless unit gives endless hot water only if the gas line, the vent and the yearly descale are all done right.

Call (847) 281-8626

A tankless water heater in Morton Grove, IL heats water only while a tap is open, passing it over a gas burner or electric element and a heat exchanger instead of storing it in a tank. That removes standby heat loss and the risk of a tank splitting open. It also adds new jobs, because the heat exchanger scales up in hard water and the gas line has to carry a bigger load.

Signs a tankless heater needs service

  • An error code on the display that returns after a reset
  • Hot water that swings between hot and lukewarm during a shower
  • Weaker hot water flow than a year ago
  • Kettle-like rumbling or ticking while the unit runs
  • A long wait for hot water, or hot water that cuts out
  • Visible white crust at the inlet screen or the connections

Flushing and descaling a tankless water heater

Scale is the main enemy of a tankless unit. The water Morton Grove receives from Evanston averages 136 mg/L of hardness in that city's 2023 report, close to 8.0 grains in each gallon. At that level, minerals bake onto the heat exchanger wall every time the burner fires. A thin layer insulates the metal, so the unit works harder to deliver the same temperature, and a thick layer narrows the passage and triggers error codes.

A flush removes the scale by circulating a mild acid through the heat exchanger. Check your manual first, since the manufacturer sets the interval and the approved cleaner. With moderately hard water, a yearly flush is a sensible starting point. A softer schedule can work if the unit runs on softened water.

How a flush is done

  1. 1
    Turn off power and gasThe unit must be off and cool before any valve is touched.
  2. 2
    Close the isolation valvesService valves on the hot and cold lines let you route flow through the unit alone.
  3. 3
    Connect the pump and hosesA small pump circulates descaling solution from a bucket through the heat exchanger and back.
  4. 4
    Circulate the solutionThe pump runs for a set time, often close to an hour, until the liquid shows no more fizzing.
  5. 5
    Rinse and restoreThe unit is flushed with clean water, the inlet screen is cleaned, the valves are reopened and the plumber checks for leaks and a clean burn.

Many homeowners can handle a flush if the unit has service valves. Without them, a plumber adds a set first. Combustion checks, error code diagnosis and any gas work belong to a licensed plumber.

Is a tankless heater right for your house?

Tankless units shine in households that run several hot water draws in sequence and in homes short on floor space. They are a weaker fit where the gas line is small, the vent run is long or the budget is tight. A tank is simpler and often the better value for a small household with modest demand.

Gas line capacity and venting

A tankless burner draws far more gas at once than a tank burner. Nicor Gas serves Morton Grove, and the pipe from the meter has to carry the new unit plus the furnace and the range at the same time. If the line is undersized, the heater shuts down or runs rough when other appliances fire, so the plumber measures the run and calculates the load before ordering.

High-efficiency units use sealed plastic vent pipe, often through a side wall. That changes where the heater can go and how the pipe is sloped. The 2018 International Fuel Gas and Mechanical Codes, which Morton Grove has adopted with local amendments, govern the details. Should you notice a gas odor near the unit, get everyone outside, then dial 911 and the emergency line printed on your gas statement.

What to do if a tankless unit smells of gas

  • Leave the building right away, and take everyone with you.
  • Do not flip switches, use a phone inside or light anything.
  • From outside, call 911 and the gas utility emergency number printed on your bill.
  • Call us after the gas company says the home is safe, so a licensed plumber can check the unit and vent.

Protecting the unit with softer water

Scale is the main wear factor on a tankless unit. A water softener cuts scale at the source and lengthens the time between flushes. At 8.0 grains per gallon, the water is moderately hard, so a softener is an option and not a requirement, and a yearly flush may be enough.

When a tankless unit fails, water heater repair covers the diagnosis side, including gas valves, igniters, flow sensors and error codes.

What moves the price of a tankless project

What changes the cost of a tankless job

  • Unit capacity and brand tier: Higher output and more features raise the equipment cost.
  • Gas pipe upgrade: A larger line from the meter adds material and labor.
  • Vent route: A longer vent run or a new wall penetration adds parts and time.
  • Condensate drain and electrical: High-efficiency units need a drain for acidic condensate and a nearby outlet.
  • Service valves and water treatment: Isolation valves for flushing and a softener both add to the first cost and cut later service.

Insurance for the home generally stays out of a planned switch to tankless, because policies respond to sudden damage and not to elective improvements. What you should expect from the plumber is a written, clear price set before the first tool comes out.
