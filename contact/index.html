Call the dispatch line

The fastest way to reach a plumber in Morton Grove is the phone. Dispatch answers at (847) 281-8626, 24/7, every day. We do not use an online form, because a form sits in a queue and a leak does not wait. Calling puts you in touch with a person who can ask the right questions right away.

Our business address is 7250 Dempster St, Morton Grove, IL 60053. Email is contact@247plumbermortongrove.com. Use email for non-urgent questions, and call for anything that is wet, backing up or smells like gas.

What to tell the dispatcher

A few details make the call shorter and help the plumber arrive with the right tools.

Have these ready when you call

  • The street address, including the town and ZIP code
  • What you see, hear or smell, and where it is in the house
  • If you have shut off the water, and if it worked
  • Any gas smell, in which case leave the building and call 911 first
  • Whether water is near outlets, the panel or the furnace
  • How a plumber can get in, such as a gate, a locked door or a pet

What happens on the call

The call flow

  1. 1
    Say what is happening.Start with the problem in plain words. The dispatcher will take it from there.
  2. 2
    Follow the safety steps.You will be told how to shut off water or stay clear of a hazard while you wait.
  3. 3
    Get the plan.A licensed plumber is assigned. We do not promise an arrival time, but you will know the plan.
  4. 4
    Agree on the price first.The plumber looks at the problem, explains it and gives a clear price before work starts.

Finding the business
