Start with what is urgent

Plumbing services in Morton Grove fall into two groups, the ones that cannot wait and the ones that can. Water pouring from a pipe, sewage in a floor drain and a leaking water heater belong in the first group. Call at any hour, because the dispatch line is open 24/7. A burst pipe repair or a sewer line cleaning done fast limits the damage to floors, drywall and stored belongings.

A dripping faucet, a slow drain in one sink or a toilet that runs after flushing can usually wait for a same-day appointment when available. Even then, shut the fixture's supply valve if it is leaking steadily.

Repair or replace

For fixtures and appliances, the question is whether a repair will last. A part that fails on a young unit is worth fixing. A part that fails on an old one often signals that other parts are close behind. Ask the plumber to explain the age of the unit, what the repair buys you and what a replacement would involve.

Water heaters show this clearly. A bad thermostat or element is a modest repair, so water heater repair makes sense. A tank that leaks from the bottom cannot be patched, and water heater replacement is the right call. Pipes work the same way. One failed joint is a repair, but pipes that keep failing in different spots may point to repiping.

How the services are grouped below

The cards that follow are grouped by problem. Drains and sewers covers clogs, hydro jetting and camera work. Water heaters covers repair, installation and tankless units. Leaks and pipes covers leak detection, burst pipes and service lines. Fixtures covers toilets, faucets and disposals, and Commercial and specialty covers business plumbing, sump pumps and water softeners.

Every job starts the same way. A licensed plumber looks at the problem, explains the cause and gives a clear price before work starts. Some jobs need a permit from the village, and the plumber will tell you when that is the case. If you cannot tell which card fits, call (847) 281-8626.
