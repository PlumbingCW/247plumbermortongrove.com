Chicago water, not Evanston water

The city buys every gallon from Chicago, which draws Lake Michigan water and filters it at the Jardine Filtration Plant. Morton Grove gets Evanston water instead. That means any hardness or water quality figure you read for Morton Grove does not apply to a Park Ridge kitchen.

For a plumber, the difference matters when sizing a water heater or deciding on a softener. If you want to know how hard your water is, check the Chicago water report for your area or ask the plumber to test it at your tap.

Rivers, rail and where the water goes

The Des Plaines River divides Park Ridge from Des Plaines, which sits on the other bank. Land near any river is lower than land farther away, and low land can take on groundwater after a long rain. If you live near the river, test the sump pump and keep drains clear before big storms.

Two Metra stations, Park Ridge and Dee Road, serve the Union Pacific Northwest line. They make useful reference points when you describe your location to dispatch, especially if your street is hard to find in the dark.

A sewer line leaves your house and joins a larger line in the street. When it clogs or cracks, water backs up into the lowest drain. A sewer camera inspection runs a small waterproof camera through the pipe, which shows roots, breaks, sags and grease before anything is dug up. Sewer inspection is the first step when drains back up more than once, because it tells you whether the next move is cleaning or repair.

What a sewer inspection finds

Cleaning treats the symptom, and the camera shows the cause. A plumber feeds the camera through a cleanout or a toilet flange, watches the live video and notes where any problem sits along the pipe.

How a camera inspection works

  1. 1
    Find accessThe plumber locates a cleanout, or pulls a toilet to reach the line.
  2. 2
    Run the cameraA flexible rod pushes the camera down the pipe while the plumber watches the screen.
  3. 3
    Mark the problemA locator at the surface marks the spot of any break or root mass.
  4. 4
    Explain the optionsThe plumber shows you the video and tells you whether cleaning, spot repair or a longer fix makes sense.

If the video shows a broken or collapsed section, sewer line repair is the next conversation. Cleaning will not fix a broken pipe, and any plumber who suggests it repeatedly is only buying time.

Water heaters that have reached the end

A tank heater has an anode rod inside that corrodes in place of the tank steel. When the rod is gone, the tank itself begins to rust, and a rusted tank will leak. Water pooling under the unit is the clearest sign that water heater replacement is the right answer and not a repair.

Replacement also makes sense when the heater is old and has needed a part twice. The honest trade-off is a bigger bill today against a repair that may fail again next month. A plumber should explain which parts are failing and ask about your household size and hot water habits before recommending a size.

Advocate Lutheran General Hospital is Park Ridge's top employer, so some households work overnight or on odd shifts. Hot water is a daily need for them, and a heater that dies at 2 a.m. cannot wait for the next business day. Calls reach a live dispatcher at all hours, and a same-day slot is possible if the schedule has one.

When to replace instead of repair

  • Water or rust stains around the base of the tank
  • A tank old enough that you cannot find a date on it
  • Two repairs within a year
  • Rust-colored hot water with clear cold water
  • Constant rumbling from sediment

Clogs that keep coming back

Frequent clogs are a sign the pipe is narrowed, damaged or invaded by roots. Drain cleaning clears a blockage, but if a line has been cleaned twice in a year, ask for the camera inspection described above. Spending on repeat cleanings without a diagnosis costs more in the long run.

Park Ridge note

Park Ridge became a city in 1873 and began as Pennyville, after George Penny, who started a brickworks in 1854. The nickname Brickton followed. Its 39,656 residents live on 7.14 square miles in Maine, Leyden and Norwood Park townships. Because the city sits in three townships, permit and records questions can differ by address, so ask the city building department which office applies to your street.

Before we arrive in Park Ridge

Do these things while you wait for the plumber:

  • Shut the main water valve when a pipe has failed or a tank has sprung a leak.
  • Stay away from any sewage that has come up through a drain, and keep pets out as well.
  • Do not run the dishwasher, washer or shower while a drain is backing up.
  • Switch off a water heater at its breaker or gas valve only if the area is dry and safe to reach.
  • Gas odor in the house means you walk out first, then phone 911 and your gas company.
